# AyurPass – Business Strategy & Monetization

## Dual-Sided Marketplace Economics

**Marketplace Commission (Consumer Bookings)**
- 15–22% platform fee on gross booking value (tiered)
  - Luxury Spa/Meditation: 18%
  - Ayurveda/Yoga consultations: 15%
  - New-client discovery bookings: 20–22%
- Minimum fee: $8–12 per transaction

**Payment Processing**
- Passed through via Stripe (~2.9% + $0.30)
- Platform takes no additional cut

**Provider Payout**
- Net amount transferred via Stripe Connect within 2–7 business days
- Instant payouts available for Enterprise providers (extra fee)

## Tiered SaaS Subscriptions for Providers

| Tier | Price | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **Free Listing** | $0 forever | Branded public practice profile (`ayurpass.com/@yourbrand`), directory search visibility, enquiry inbox, iOS & Android listing |
| **Growth** | $369/mo | **Unlimited appointments & recurring series** (weekly/bi-weekly/monthly), **2-Way Calendar Sync** (Google Calendar & Apple iCal), **Block customers** (CRM protection against no-shows/fraud), **Accept payments & deposits** (Stripe Connect), **Branded Booking Page**, **Automated email reminders & confirmations** (24h & 2h), **Integrations with leading apps** (Square POS, Stripe, Mailchimp), **iOS & Android apps** for staff/clients, **Team collaboration tools** (multi-staff calendars, rooms, permissions), analytics & client CRM |
| **Enterprise** | Custom ($799+/mo) | Everything in Growth across multi-location practices, **Dedicated Branded Mobile App** (custom app store listing & app icon), custom domains & white-label web embeds, custom API/webhooks, dedicated partner manager, priority payouts |

## Additional Revenue Streams
- Product marketplace commissions (10–15%)
- Premium listing boosts
- Virtual consultation fee share
- Anonymized data insights
- White-label solutions for hotel chains

## Compliance Framework
- **HIPAA** (US): Full PHI handling with BAA, encryption, RBAC, audit logging
- **GDPR** (EU): Explicit consent, data residency, DPO, DPIAs
- **Global**: PIPEDA, PDPA, SOC 2 Type II + HIPAA audits

## 3-Tier User Acquisition Strategy
1. **Premium Consumers** – Meta/Google/Instagram ads + influencer partnerships + referral program
2. **Boutique Owners & Professionals** – Outbound sales + 30-day free trial + hotel partnerships
3. **Scale & Virality** – SEO, ASO, PR, community features, yoga teacher training partnerships

**Target**: CAC payback < 6 months with strong retention via AI routines and loyalty programs.
