# AyurPass – Additional High-Value Features

## Recommended Capabilities

### AI Treatment Plan Generator
- Vedic-knowledge fine-tuned model + rules engine
- Drafts plans for professional review

### Wearable Integration
- Oura, Whoop, Apple Health, Garmin
- Pull objective data (sleep, HRV, stress) to correlate with dosha balance

### Secure Document Vault
- Client-uploaded reports with OCR and professional annotation (consent-gated)

### Loyalty & Progress Rewards
- Points for adherence, redeemable for services/products
- “Dosha Balance Score” gamification

### Provider Collaboration Tools
- Secure messaging and joint plan editing between Vaidya + Yoga instructor (client approval required)

### Multi-Language + Vedic Terminology
- Full support for Sanskrit terms with explanations

### Group & Corporate Wellness
- Team packages, corporate retreats, B2B dashboards

### Supply Chain Traceability (Products)
- Blockchain-light verification of authentic Ayurvedic sourcing

### Post-Treatment Integration Protocols
- Automated follow-up plans to maintain benefits after retreats or intensive treatments

---

These features dramatically increase lifetime value, retention, and professional stickiness while maintaining the highest standards of privacy and Vedic authenticity.
